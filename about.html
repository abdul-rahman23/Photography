Meet Malcolm Lismore
Passionate Lensman | Nature Enthusiast

Welcome to the world of Malcolm Lismore, a freelance photographer with a heart tethered to the untamed beauty of the North West coast of Scotland. Malcolm's passion for photography began at a young age, growing up surrounded by the beauty of the Scottish Highlands. This passion is evident in his work, which primarily focuses on capturing the stunning landscapes and wildlife of his home, and the timeless moments of life's special occasions.
